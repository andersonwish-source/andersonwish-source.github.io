本程式僅供擁有者本人使用，透過 Google 授權讀取擁有者本人的行事曆與信件，用來提醒行程與整理信件。

This app is for the owner's personal use only. Google data is used solely to provide calendar reminders and email organization for the owner, is never sold or shared with third parties, and access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings.
